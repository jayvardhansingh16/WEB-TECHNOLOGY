Welcome to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is computer technology that lets machines think, learn, and solve problems like humans. It includes generative text, deep learning, and everyday tools like search engines and maps

An artificial intelligence article is a written piece or publication that explains, analyzes, or reports on computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intellect, such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving. These texts can range from encyclopedia entries to news reports on machine learning breakthroughs.
