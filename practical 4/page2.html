More About Artificial Intelligence

Key Concepts in AI Machine Learning (ML): Systems learn from data and get better over time without being directly programmed for every task.Deep Learning: Uses artificial neural networks to mimic human brain processes for complex tasks like speech and image recognition.Natural Language Processing (NLP): Helps computers read, understand, and talk back using human language.Generative AI: Creates new text, images, music, or code based on patterns learned from massive datasets.Levels of AINarrow AI: Systems built to do one specific job well, such as translate languages or play chess (this is what we use today).General AI (AGI): A theoretical future level where machines match human intelligence across all domains.Super AI: A hypothetical future stage where machine intelligence far surpasses human capability
