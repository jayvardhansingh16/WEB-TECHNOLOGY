Importance of Commerce

Commerce plays an important role in the development of the economy. It includes the buying and selling of goods and services, banking, insurance, and transportation. Studying commerce helps students develop financial knowledge, communication skills, and business understanding. It also provides many opportunities for employment and entrepreneurship.
