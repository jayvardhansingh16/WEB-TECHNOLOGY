About My Major

I am studying Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com). Commerce is a field that focuses on business, accounting, finance, economics, and management. It helps students understand how businesses work and how money is managed.

Subjects in Commerce

  • Accounting
  • Business Management
  • Economics
  • Financial Management
  • Business Law

Career Opportunities

After completing B.Com, students can work in banking, accounting, finance, taxation, and business. They can also pursue higher studies such as M.Com, MBA, or professional courses.
